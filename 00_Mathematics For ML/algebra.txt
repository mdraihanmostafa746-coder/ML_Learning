#### LINEAR REGRESSION ####

Let's You are a manager of a company and You give salary an employee 500/day and 1000 joining bonus that is fixed for everyone 

An employee's salary 

    500/day 
    1000 joining bonus ( Fixed for everyone)

Day -----> Income

0           1000
1           1500
2           2000
3           3000

equation -> 
    
    y = mx + b

Here, 
    y = final predict salary, - Output / Result
    x = working days, - Input 
    m = 500/day, rate of change 
    b = 1000 joining bonus - constant 
        Here,
            when x = 0, b = 1000

Here, 
Standered equation 
            y = 500 * x + 1000

Let's 
    one employee work 4 days then the predicted value of that employee is 

    y = 500 * 4 + 1000
    y = 3000
:. predict salary (y) of that employee is 3000
