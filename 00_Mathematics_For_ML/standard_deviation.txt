### Standard Deviation ###

    Measuring the spread of data around the mean.


Low Standard Deviation --> All value are close 

High Standard Deviation --> All  value are very fer from each other 

Class A : 

        [60, 61, 63, 59, 60] --> LSD

Class B : 

        [30, 100, 70, 20, 50] --> HSD
