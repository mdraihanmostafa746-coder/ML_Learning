### Mean , Median & Mode ###

mean = (sum of total number)/ how many numbers are there

median = middle value
    if odd
        median = middle vale 
    else even
        median = average of middle two values

mode = most frequent number

Question : 

series = 50, 70, 70, 90, 45, 76, 99, 88

Find mean, median, mode of this series

Mean = (50+70+70+90+45+76+99+88)/8
     = 588/8
     = 73.5 (Mean)

Median = (90+45)/2 
       = 135/2
       = 67.2

Mode = 70 ( Most frequent number in this series )
